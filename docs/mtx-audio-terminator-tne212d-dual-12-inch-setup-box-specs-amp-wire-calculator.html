Frequently Asked Questions (MTX Audio Terminator TNE212D Dual 12-Inch Setup)

What is the best ported box volume and tuning for the MTX Audio Terminator TNE212D Dual 12-Inch Setup?

For deep daily musical bass, build a 2.4 cubic feet net internal volume enclosure tuned to 36 Hz with 34 sq. in. of port area (24.6 inches port length). If you prefer a compact sealed trunk box, use 2.0 cubic feet net.

What size amplifier and wire gauge do I need for the MTX Audio Terminator TNE212D Dual 12-Inch Setup?

Pair the MTX Audio Terminator TNE212D Dual 12-Inch Setup with a 1-Ohm or 2-Ohm stable Class-D monoblock amplifier delivering 400W to 500W RMS. Because it draws ~36 Amps DC at full tilt, run 8 AWG or 4 AWG 100% Oxygen-Free Copper (OFC) power and ground cable with a 40A ANL fuse.

How do I set the amplifier gain voltage for 400W RMS with a multimeter?

Using Ohm's Law ($V = \sqrt{\text{Watts} \times \text{Ohms}}$), at a 1-Ohm final load your target AC speaker terminal voltage is 20.0V AC; at a 2-Ohm final load, set gain to 28.3V AC using a 40 Hz test tone.
