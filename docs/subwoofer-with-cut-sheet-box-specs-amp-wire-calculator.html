Frequently Asked Questions (Subwoofer With Cut Sheet)

What is the best ported box volume and tuning for the Subwoofer With Cut Sheet?

For deep daily musical bass, build a 1.85 cubic feet net internal volume enclosure tuned to 33 Hz with 26 sq. in. of port area (30.6 inches port length). If you prefer a compact sealed trunk box, use 1.11 cubic feet net.

What size amplifier and wire gauge do I need for the Subwoofer With Cut Sheet?

Pair the Subwoofer With Cut Sheet with a 1-Ohm or 2-Ohm stable Class-D monoblock amplifier delivering 800W to 1000W RMS. Because it draws ~72 Amps DC at full tilt, run 4 AWG 100% Oxygen-Free Copper (OFC) power and ground cable with a 90A ANL fuse.

How do I set the amplifier gain voltage for 800W RMS with a multimeter?

Using Ohm's Law ($V = \sqrt{\text{Watts} \times \text{Ohms}}$), at a 1-Ohm final load your target AC speaker terminal voltage is 28.3V AC; at a 2-Ohm final load, set gain to 40.0V AC using a 40 Hz test tone.
